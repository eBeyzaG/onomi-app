Privacy Policy

Onomi · Effective 8 August 2026

Onomi does not collect your personal data. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics and no tracking. Your photos are analysed on your phone and never leave it. Your words stay on your phone.

Photos and the camera

When you take a picture in Onomi, the image is analysed on your device using Apple's built-in Vision framework to work out what objects are in it. That analysis happens entirely on your phone. The photo is never uploaded, never sent to us, and never sent to anyone else.

A temporary copy of the picture is written to Onomi's own private storage area on your device so it can be shown on screen while you tap the labels. It is not added to your photo library, and it is removed when iOS reclaims that space or when you delete the app. We never receive it.

Your words

The words you collect, your review history and your progress toward the weekly goals are stored in a database file on your device only. None of it is sent anywhere, and we have no way to see it. Deleting the app deletes all of it permanently, so there is no copy for us or anyone else to hold.

Pronunciation

Spoken pronunciation uses Apple's speech synthesis built into iOS. No recording is made and your microphone is never used — Onomi does not request microphone access at all.

Purchases

Onomi Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple. All payment is handled by Apple; we never see or receive your card details, billing address or Apple ID.

To know whether your subscription is active, and to let you restore it on a new phone, we use RevenueCat, Inc. as a service provider. RevenueCat receives:

WhatWhy
A randomly generated, anonymous identifier for the install To match a purchase to the app without identifying you
Your App Store purchase receipt and subscription status To confirm the subscription is valid and unlock Pro features
Device platform, app version, and country inferred from IP address To process the purchase correctly and produce anonymous sales totals

This identifier is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID. RevenueCat processes this information on our behalf; their privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

What Onomi does not do

  • No accounts, logins, names or email addresses
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking of any kind
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No location access
  • No microphone or photo library access
  • No selling or sharing of data — there is none to sell
  • No tracking of you across other apps or websites

Children

Onomi is rated 4+ and is suitable for children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either. We do not knowingly gather personal data from children of any age.

Your control

You can remove any word from your deck inside the app, and deleting Onomi removes every word, photo and record it ever created on your device. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or erase on request. Your subscription is managed by Apple, under Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date. Onomi collects nothing today, and any change that altered that would be announced in the app before it took effect.

Contact
